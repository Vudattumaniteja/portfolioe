What services does Manitejs' AI Agency provide?

Manitejs provides expert custom n8n workflow creation, website creation, UI/UX designing, and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) solutions. He builds multi-agent pipelines syncing lead routing, AI content engines, and SEO monitors.

How does n8n automation improve operations?

n8n automation automates repetitive digital business pipelines, routing leads, publishing dynamic content via Gemini, tracking citations in AI search systems, and synchronizing customer CRMs.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) optimizes websites for AI retrieval systems like Gemini and ChatGPT. It ensures your branding, services, and citations rank highly in RAG search outputs.
